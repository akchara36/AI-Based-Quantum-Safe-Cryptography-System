Classical RSA vs. Post-Quantum

Classical (RSA / ECC)

Relies on integer factorization or discrete logarithms.

Vulnerable to: Shor's Algorithm running on a sufficiently powerful Quantum Computer, breaking the encryption in polynomial time.

Post-Quantum (Lattice-Based)

Relies on Learning With Errors (LWE) over lattices.

Resistant to: Both classical and quantum attacks. Finding the shortest vector in a multidimensional lattice remains extremely difficult even for quantum computers.
