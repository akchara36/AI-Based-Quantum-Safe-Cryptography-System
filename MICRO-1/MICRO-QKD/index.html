Key Concepts

  • Measurement Collapse: Measuring a photon using the wrong basis irrecoverably destroys its state into a random bit.
  • Public Comparison: Alice and Bob publicly share their bases, but rigidly hide their bits.
  • Sample Verification: A subset of bits is publicly revealed and then discarded to blindly check for Eve-induced errors.
